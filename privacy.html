Honna

Privacy policy

Effective October 9, 2026

In short: everything you enter in Honna stays on your device. There's no account, no server that stores your data, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your cycle, wellbeing and activity data are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Who we are

Honna is a workout and wellbeing tracker made by an independent developer ("we"). Questions about this policy: contact the developer through github.com/Bahanos12.

What Honna stores, and where

Honna saves what you enter in your device's own app storage. We don't receive it, and we have no copy of it.

DataExamples
Profile and settingsYour name, units, colour theme and, if you choose, your sex (used to show the right features and plans)
TrainingYour plans, including plans you import from a file, plus your workouts, sets, weights and personal bests
Menstrual cycle (optional, off unless you turn it on)Period dates and flow, cycle and period length, regularity and, if you choose, contraception type
Wellbeing (optional)Daily mood, energy, stress, sleep and symptoms
Activity (optional)Daily steps and distance, entered by you or read from your health app (see below)

Honna uses this data only on your device, to show your plan, history, cycle, suggestions and activity. Suggestions are calculated on your device; nothing is sent anywhere to compute them.

Health Connect and Apple Health

If you connect a health app (where Honna supports it), Honna asks for read-only access to your step count and walking + running distance, and nothing else. It never writes to your health app. The data is stored on your device, used only to show your activity, steps goal and history, and is never shared, sold, or used for advertising. You can disconnect at any time in Honna or in your health app's permission settings.

The use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we share

Nothing. We don't sell, rent or share your data, and we don't use it for advertising. We don't use analytics, crash reporting or advertising tools, or any third-party code that collects data about you.

When Honna connects to the internet

  • Loading the website version. If you use Honna in a browser, its files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub receives standard technical information such as your IP address (see GitHub's privacy statement). The installed app contains its files and doesn't need this.
  • Importing a plan from a file. To read PDF, spreadsheet or scanned files, Honna downloads open-source reading tools (pdf.js, SheetJS, Tesseract.js) from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr. These services see a normal download request. Your file itself is read on your device and is never uploaded.
  • Links you tap. Sources in "What the research says" open the publisher's website in your browser.

Notifications

If you allow them, rest-timer notifications are created on your device. No notification service receives your data.

Backups

"Export backup" creates a file on your device that you control. Honna never uploads it. Keep it somewhere safe if it contains cycle or wellbeing data.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Cycle, wellbeing, activity and readiness features are optional and can be turned off at any time in Profile or Activity. Turning a feature off keeps what's saved; you can delete individual entries in the app.
  • To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage (on the website: clear this site's data in your browser settings). Because we hold no copy, there's nothing for us to delete on our side, and we can't recover data you delete.

Security

Your data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself. Anyone who can unlock your device can open Honna, so use your phone's screen lock to keep it private.

Children

Honna isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. As we collect no data at all, nothing about children reaches us.

Not medical advice

Honna's suggestions, cycle estimates and research tips are general information, not medical advice, diagnosis or contraception. Talk to a doctor about health concerns.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app.
